Account deletion

Delete your account

HookScore supports self-service account deletion in the app and an email request channel for users who cannot sign in.

Delete in the app

  1. Open HookScore and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account, then confirm the deletion.

Email request if you cannot sign in

  1. Email penkin.yauhen@gmail.com from the email address used for your HookScore account.
  2. Use the subject line "HookScore account deletion request".
  3. We will confirm account ownership by email before deleting access and data.

You do not need to be signed in to send this email request.

Signed in with Apple?

HookScore revokes its Sign in with Apple access with Apple before it deletes the account. If HookScore has no Apple token for your account, the app asks you to confirm with Apple once more. If Apple does not confirm the revocation, nothing is deleted and you can try again. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for HookScore at any time in your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple.

Data deleted

  • HookScore account identity and authentication access, including Google and Apple sign-in links.
  • Saved hook score history tied to the account.
  • Daily analyzer usage records, bonus checks, and promo, creator, and invite code redemptions.
  • Your personal invite code.
  • Usage analytics events and the install source linked to the account.
  • Stored Sign in with Apple tokens, after they are revoked with Apple.

Data that may be retained

A record that the deletion was requested and completed (account email and timestamps) is kept as proof of the deletion. AI results you reported stay available for safety review without your account identifier or email address. Single-use promo codes stay marked as used without a link to your account. Provider logs and backups at Supabase, Google, or OpenAI may persist for a limited period under their retention practices.
