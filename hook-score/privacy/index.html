Privacy Policy

HookScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what HookScore collects and processes when you create an account, use a free guest check, or ask the app to score a short-form video hook. HookScore does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information We Collect

Account and Supabase auth data

HookScore uses Supabase for authentication. When you register or log in with email, Supabase processes your email address, password authentication data, Supabase user identifier, session identifiers, and the name you provide during registration. HookScore uses that information to create your account, keep you signed in, and associate saved hook checks with your account.

If you choose to continue with Google or Apple, the selected provider confirms your identity and shares your account identifier, email address, and, where you allow it, your name with Supabase. With Apple you can hide your real email address, in which case HookScore receives an Apple relay address. HookScore does not receive your Google or Apple password and uses this information only to create your account and keep you signed in.

When you sign in with Apple, HookScore's server also keeps the Sign in with Apple refresh token that Apple issues for your account. The app hands the Apple credential to the server once, right after you sign in, and does not keep it. The token is stored only on the server and is used for one purpose: to revoke HookScore's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account. HookScore does not use it to read data from your Apple account.

Hook check content

When you score a hook, HookScore processes the text and context you enter, including the hook text, video description, target audience, niche, selected goals, selected output language, and score results returned by the analyzer.

Microphone dictation

HookScore can use your microphone only when you choose the Dictate action in a text field. Your speech is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, the system speech recognizer on Android). Depending on your device and settings, that service may process the audio on the device or send it to Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. HookScore receives only the resulting text, which you can edit, and does not record or store dictation audio.

Selected video, frame, and audio processing

Adding a video is optional. If you select a video, HookScore samples the opening window you choose, currently three to five seconds. The app extracts still frames from that opening window and sends those image samples to the AI provider. On web builds, if the provider supports audio input, the app may also extract a short WAV audio sample from the same opening window and send it to that provider. The iOS and Android apps send image frames only. The full video file is never uploaded.

HookScore stores video metadata for authenticated history, such as filename, MIME type, local source URI, selected opening window, preparation time, and duration. HookScore does not store the raw selected video file, extracted frame images, or extracted audio samples in its Supabase history tables.

Usage, history, credits, and codes

For authenticated users, HookScore stores daily usage counts and saved hook history in Supabase. Saved history includes the analysis result, score, goals, model metadata, hook context, selected clip metadata, and timestamps. Guest checks are not saved to account history.

If you redeem a promo, creator, or invite code, HookScore stores which code you redeemed, when, and how many bonus checks it added, together with your bonus check balance. Every account can get a personal invite code; HookScore stores that code and how many people redeemed it so it can reward you. The person who shares an invite code sees only how many friends joined, not who they are.

Score share cards

If you tap Share on a result, the app renders the score card as an image on your device and opens your device's share sheet. The image is shared only with the app or person you choose. HookScore does not upload or store the shared card.

Usage analytics

The app records a small set of in-app events (for example: app opened, hook check started or completed with a score range, code applied, score card or invite shared, invite link opened) together with the app's random device UUID, your account identifier when you are signed in, platform, app version and device language. Events never include your hook text, video, email or other free text, and never include the invite code you share. HookScore stores them in its own Supabase database and uses them only to understand which features and channels work; no third-party analytics service receives them. Raw events are deleted after 180 days; only daily totals are kept. You can turn usage analytics off in Settings. Events linked to your account are deleted when you delete the account.

Install source and invite links

On Android the app reads the Google Play install referrer once to learn which link you installed from (for example "tiktok" or a creator code) and stores that channel with your account. HookScore keeps only the channel name, campaign and creator code, not the full referrer. If you open a HookScore invite or creator link, the app remembers the code and its channel on your device until you sign in, then applies the code and stores the channel with your account. On iOS the install source is not available to the app.

Store ratings

The app may ask Apple or Google to show their rating prompt after a few successful checks. The prompt is shown by the store, and HookScore does not receive your rating or review through it.

Result reports

If you report an AI-generated hook result from inside the app, HookScore stores the reported result identifier, the result content, report reason, timestamp, and, when available, your account identifier and email address. HookScore uses these reports to review analyzer quality, safety, and policy compliance.

Guest device UUID

If you use the free guest checks, HookScore creates a random device UUID and stores it locally on your device. On web, it is stored in browser local storage. On native builds, it is stored in secure device storage. Supabase stores that UUID with guest usage count, first seen time, and last used time so HookScore can enforce the guest check limit. The native apps also send the same UUID with usage analytics events; after you sign in, it is stored together with your account's events, so HookScore can tell that guest checks and a later sign-up happened on the same device. The UUID is not an advertising identifier or a device hardware identifier.

AI Provider Usage

Hook analysis runs through a HookScore server function on Supabase, which calls the AI provider with server-side credentials. HookScore currently uses the Google Gemini API. The server can also be configured to use OpenAI; this policy will be updated before that becomes the default. The provider receives the hook text and context you enter, sampled frames from an optional selected video, and, when supported and available, a short audio sample from the selected opening window. The provider returns structured analysis, scores, observations, and rewrite suggestions. Your name, email address, and account identifier are not sent to the AI provider.

Before the first analysis on a device, the app explains what will be sent and that it goes to the Google Gemini AI service, and asks for your permission. Nothing is sent to the AI provider unless you agree. If you choose "Not now", no analysis is run.

HookScore uses the paid Gemini API. Under Google's terms for paid Gemini API services, Google processes this data as a service provider under its data processing terms, does not use it to improve Google products, and keeps it only for a limited time to detect abuse. These terms give the data protection equal to the protection described in this policy. HookScore sends only the information needed to generate the hook score.

Service Providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and server functions.
  • Google: Gemini API for hook analysis, and Google sign-in if you choose it.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple if you choose it.
  • OpenAI: only if the analyzer is switched to OpenAI, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate accounts and keep sessions active.
  • Generate hook scores, observations, and rewrite suggestions.
  • Show saved history for authenticated accounts.
  • Apply daily limits, guest limits, bonus checks, and promo, creator, and invite codes.
  • Review reported AI-generated results and improve safety controls.
  • Understand which features and acquisition channels work, using usage analytics and the install source.
  • Revoke Sign in with Apple access when you delete your account.
  • Debug service reliability and protect against misuse.

Retention and Deletion

Authenticated hook history, usage records, bonus checks, and code redemptions are retained while your account remains active. Guest UUID usage records are retained as needed to enforce the guest check limit. Raw usage analytics events are deleted after 180 days; after that only daily totals per event and platform, without identifiers, are kept. Local guest UUID storage remains on your device until you clear app data or browser storage.

You can delete your HookScore account at any time from Settings in the app. If you signed in with Apple, HookScore first revokes its Sign in with Apple access with Apple. If Apple does not confirm the revocation, the account is not deleted and the app asks you to try again. HookScore then deletes your account and the data linked to it: saved history, usage records, bonus checks, code redemptions, your invite code, usage analytics events and install source linked to the account, and stored Apple tokens. Events recorded with only the guest device UUID before you signed in are no longer linked to you and are deleted after 180 days. A record that a deletion was requested and completed, with the account email and timestamps, is kept as proof of the deletion. Reported results stay available for safety review without your account identifier or email address.

If you cannot sign in, you can request deletion of your account data or guest usage record by emailing the contact address below. Some provider-side logs or backups may remain for a limited period according to Supabase, Google, or OpenAI retention practices.

Children

HookScore is intended for creators and businesses and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
